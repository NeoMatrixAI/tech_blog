Published posts from **Axiom**, the systems and infrastructure team at NeoMatrix. Data collection, backtesting, live execution, results analysis.

Each post takes one thing that actually happened and answers four questions: what broke, how we found it, what we tried that didn't work, and how we fixed it. Then the one most writeups skip — what's still unresolved.

Long-form posts go out on Substack, with short threads on X.

## Contents

Published articles live in [`published/`](published/), numbered in publication order, with their images alongside. Posts are added here as they go out.
